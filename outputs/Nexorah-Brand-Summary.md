## 2. Who we are

Nexorah helps businesses get found, trusted and chosen online. We don't just "sell websites". We find what's costing a business customers (they can't find you, they don't trust what they see, or your team is drowning in messages and spreadsheets) and fix it together with a website, a web app, a booking system or an automation.

**Mission:** give small and growing businesses the same quality of digital tools that big companies have, built fast with no-code and AI.

**Brand voice:** a consultant, not a salesman.
- Lead with the customer's problem, not our features
- Ask questions before giving answers
- Say "we / us / our" and "here's what I'd recommend…"
- Honest, even when the answer is "you don't need a website yet"
- Simple, clear English with a little warmth and humour

## 9. Launch campaign: "5 Free Websites" (2–16 Oct 2026)

**Offer:** 5 free websites, up to 3 pages each. The client pays only for domain and hosting, and gives an honest review and portfolio rights in return.
**CTA:** DM "FREE" or WhatsApp +234 906 906 0835
**Schedule:** 3 posts a day (8 AM · 1 PM · 7 PM Lagos time) on Instagram, LinkedIn and X, through Buffer

**Story arc:** launch & offer → one audience per day (small business, restaurants, real estate, coaches, startups, bigger businesses) → trust (reviews, meet George, FAQ) → countdown & last call.

**Relaunch (9 Oct):** rewritten in a consultant voice inspired by Brian Tracy's *Be a Sales Superstar*.
**Comment-first rewrite (12 Oct):** every post asks for an easy reply (a number, a letter, one word or a keyword such as FREE or AUDIT).

### Final results (2–16 Oct)

| Platform | Posts | Reach / impressions | Comments | Reactions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 30 (+4 failed during an early disconnect) | Not reported by Buffer | **18** | 2 |
| LinkedIn | 32 | 269 impressions · 108 people | 1 | 2 |
| X | 32 | 81 impressions | 0 | 0 |

*Figures from Buffer on 17 Oct. Totals may include a couple of older profile posts.*

### Lessons learned
1. **Instagram is our strongest channel.** It produced almost every comment.
2. **Easy-answer posts win.** "Rate 1–10" and "finish the sentence" beat open questions.
3. **Reach is the bottleneck, not copy.** LinkedIn and X were seen by very few people. Grow the audience with daily comments, direct DMs, WhatsApp Status and shares.
4. **Check channel connections daily.** Instagram disconnected on 3–5 Oct and 4 posts failed.
5. **Buffer's free plan holds only 10 queued posts per channel**, so top up every 3 days or use a paid plan.
